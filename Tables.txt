Oo. Gawin nating **hierarchical ang category ng Parts, Services, at Tools** gamit ang `Parent...CategoryId`, para hindi na kailangan ng hiwalay na `Subcategory` table.

```text
ServiceCategory
Preventive Maintenance
└── Oil & Fluid Services
    └── Oil Change
```

Same concept sa Parts at Tools.

# Final hierarchy ng tatlong catalog

Ito na ngayon ang consistent structure:

```text
PARTS

PartCategory
│
├── Brake System
│   ├── Brake Pads
│   │     ↓
│   │   Part
│   │   Ceramic Brake Pads
│   │
│   └── Brake Rotors
│         ↓
│       Part
│       Front Brake Rotor
```

```text
SERVICES

ServiceCategory
│
├── Preventive Maintenance
│   ├── Oil & Fluid Services
│   │       ↓
│   │     Service
│   │     Synthetic Oil Change
│   │
│   └── Filter Services
│           ↓
│         Service
│         Air Filter Replacement
```

```text
TOOLS

ToolCategory
│
├── Diagnostic Equipment
│   ├── OBD Scanners
│   │       ↓
│   │     Tool
│   │     Autel Scanner
│   │
│   └── Battery Testers
│           ↓
│         Tool
│         Digital Battery Tester
```

So **41 business/support tables** ngayon sa consolidated structure natin, excluding the standard ASP.NET Identity tables such as `AspNetUsers`, `AspNetRoles`, `AspNetUserRoles`, etc.

At ang magandang part ng `ParentCategoryId` approach: kung someday gusto mong:

```text
Category
  ↓
Subcategory
  ↓
Sub-subcategory
  ↓
Item
```

**wala ka nang bagong table na gagawin.** Same category table lang ang gagamitin recursively.
